# 1. Purpose

This document defines the complete end-to-end business workflow of the Pharmacy Invoice Automation System.

The workflow describes how purchase invoices are processed from the moment they are received until all invoice data has been entered into the pharmacy management system.

This document defines **business behavior only**.

Implementation details are defined in later technical documents.

---

# 2. Business Goal

The system shall:

- Reduce manual data entry.
- Increase processing speed.
- Minimize human errors.
- Maintain business accuracy.
- Support very large numbers of invoices.
- Allow human verification before final submission.

---


# 4. Detailed Workflow

## Step 1 — Receive Invoice

### Input

Supplier purchase invoice.

Possible formats:

- PDF
- Image
- Scanned document

### Output

Raw invoice file stored inside the project.

---

## Step 2 — Organize Invoice Files

Invoices are grouped by:

- Customer
- Reporting period
- Supplier

Every invoice receives a unique internal identifier.

Output:

Invoice ready for OCR.

---

## Step 3 — OCR Processing

OCR extracts all visible text from the invoice.

OCR responsibilities:

- Preserve layout.
- Preserve numbers.
- Preserve dates.
- Preserve medicine names.

OCR does not interpret business meaning.

Output:

Raw OCR text.

---

## Step 4 — AI Extraction

AI converts OCR text into structured business data.

Typical fields include:

Invoice

- Invoice Number
- Invoice Date
- Supplier
- Tax Code

Purchase Items

- Medicine Name
- Unit
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- VAT
- Batch Number
- Expiry Date

Output:

Structured invoice data.

---

## Step 5 — Business Validation

The extracted data is validated against Business Rules.

Validation includes:

- Required fields
- Date validation
- Quantity validation
- Price validation
- Supplier validation
- Medicine validation

Validation does not modify data.

Output:

Validated invoice.

or

Validation errors.

---

## Step 6 — Human Review

Human operators review only invoices that require attention.

Typical reasons include:

- Unknown supplier
- Unknown medicine
- Missing fields
- OCR uncertainty
- AI uncertainty
- Validation failure

Operators may:

- Approve
- Correct
- Reject

Approved values become the source of truth.

---

## Step 7 — Generate Approved Dataset

After approval:

A normalized dataset is generated.

This dataset becomes the only source used by browser automation.

Neither OCR output nor raw AI output shall be used directly.

---

## Step 8 — Browser Automation

The automation system logs into the pharmacy management website.

For every approved invoice:

- Open Purchase Invoice screen.
- Fill supplier information.
- Fill invoice information.
- Add purchase items.
- Save invoice.

Automation must never change approved data.

---

## Step 9 — Verification

The system verifies that the entered information matches the approved dataset.

Verification may include:

- Supplier
- Invoice Number
- Item Count
- Total Amount

Verification failures require manual review.

---

## Step 10 — Invoice Completed

Processing finishes successfully when:

- Invoice entered.
- Verification passed.
- Audit log recorded.

Invoice status becomes:

Completed.

---

# 5. Human Decision Points

Human intervention is required when:

- Supplier cannot be identified.
- Medicine cannot be matched.
- OCR confidence is too low.
- AI confidence is too low.
- Business validation fails.
- Browser automation fails repeatedly.

---

# 6. Business Principles

The following principles always apply.

Business correctness has higher priority than automation speed.

Human approval overrides AI output.

Automation never invents business data.

Automation only processes approved datasets.

Every business decision must be auditable.

---

# 8. Expected Processing Result

Each successfully processed invoice shall produce:

- Approved invoice record.
- Approved purchase items.
- Complete audit log.
- Browser automation log.
- Processing status.
- Error log (if applicable).

---

# 9. Success Criteria

A workflow execution is considered successful when:

- OCR completes.
- AI extraction completes.
- Business validation passes.
- Human review (if required) is completed.
- Browser automation succeeds.
- Verification succeeds.
- Invoice status is Completed.
