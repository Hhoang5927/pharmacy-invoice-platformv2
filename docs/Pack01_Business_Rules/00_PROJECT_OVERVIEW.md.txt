# Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of the Pharmacy Invoice Automation System.

It explains:

- Why the project exists
- What business problem it solves

- What is inside the project scope
- What is intentionally outside the project scope

---

# Executive Summary

The Pharmacy Invoice Automation System is an internal software platform designed to automate the processing of pharmaceutical supplier purchase invoices.

Instead of manually reading every invoice and entering hundreds of medicine lines into the pharmacy management website, the system assists the operator by automating most of the repetitive work while keeping a human reviewer in control of the final result.

The project combines OCR, AI-assisted data extraction, business validation, Excel generation and browser automation into a single end-to-end workflow.

---

# Business Background

A pharmacy regularly receives purchase invoices from multiple suppliers.

Each supplier may use different invoice formats.

Each invoice can contain dozens or even hundreds of medicine items.

A typical accounting project may involve:

- Hundreds of invoices
- Three years of historical records
- Tens of thousands of medicine lines

Entering this data manually is:

- Slow
- Repetitive
- Error-prone
- Difficult to audit

The purpose of this project is to significantly reduce manual work while maintaining high accuracy.

---

# Problem Statement

Current manual workflow has several limitations:

## Manual Reading

Operators must inspect every invoice manually.

---

## Manual Typing

Every medicine must be entered one by one.

---

## Human Error

Common mistakes include:

- Incorrect quantity
- Incorrect unit price
- Wrong VAT
- Missing batch number
- Wrong expiry date
- Incorrect supplier

---

## Low Productivity

Processing hundreds of invoices may require several working days.

---

## Difficult Quality Control

Finding mistakes after manual entry is expensive and time-consuming.

# Intended Users

Primary users:

- Pharmacy owner
- Pharmacy staff
- Accounting staff

Future users may include:

- Multiple pharmacy branches
- Accounting service providers
- Enterprise pharmacy groups

---

# System Scope

The system SHALL:

- Read supplier invoices
- Extract invoice information
- Validate extracted data
- Detect inconsistencies
- Allow manual review
- Generate intermediate Excel files
- Automatically enter validated data into the pharmacy management website
- Produce processing reports

---

# Out of Scope

The system SHALL NOT:

- Replace accounting software
- Replace pharmacy management software
- Generate tax declarations
- Perform financial accounting
- Manage inventory
- Replace human approval

# Key Features

## Invoice Processing

Support multiple supplier invoice formats.

---

## OCR

Extract raw text from scanned invoices.

---

## AI Extraction

Convert OCR text into structured business data.

---

## Business Validation

Verify extracted values against business rules.

---

## Human Review

Allow users to inspect and correct extracted information before automation.

---

## Excel Generation

Produce a structured intermediate dataset.

This Excel file serves as the canonical input for browser automation.

---

## Browser Automation

Automatically input validated data into the pharmacy management website.

---

## Reporting

Generate processing summaries and error reports.

---

# Design Principles

The project follows several guiding principles.

## Accuracy First

Correct results are more important than maximum speed.

---

## Human in the Loop

Humans always make the final decision.

---

## Traceability

Every important action must be auditable.

---

## Recoverability

Interrupted processing should be resumable.

---

## Extensibility

The system should support future enhancements with minimal redesign.

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it can:

- Process large batches of supplier invoices reliably
- Reduce manual typing significantly
- Maintain a high level of accuracy
- Recover gracefully from failures
- Produce reproducible results
- Be maintainable for many years

---

# Assumptions

The project assumes:

- Supplier invoices are legally valid documents.
- OCR quality depends on scan quality.
- AI extraction may occasionally require manual correction.
- Internet access is available for browser automation.
- Human review remains part of the workflow.

---

# Risks

Potential risks include:

- Poor scan quality
- OCR errors
- AI hallucination
- Website UI changes
- Slow internet connection
- Browser automation failures

Mitigation strategies are defined in later documents.
