>

> Owner: Product Owner

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines all business rules related to purchase invoice processing.

Every purchase invoice must pass these rules before being approved for browser automation.

---

# 2. Scope

Applies to:

- OCR
- AI Extraction
- Business Validation
- Human Review
- Excel Dataset
- Browser Automation

---

# 3. Business Objective

The system shall:

- correctly identify invoices
- preserve invoice information
- prevent duplicate invoices
- validate required information
- generate one approved invoice dataset

---

# 4. Invoice Data Model

Invoice Header

Required

- Invoice Number
- Invoice Date
- Supplier
- Purchase Items

Optional

- Notes

---

Purchase Item

Required

- Medicine Name
- Quantity
- Unit
- Unit Price
- VAT

Optional

- Batch Number
- Expiry Date

---

# 5. Invoice Lifecycle

```

Invoice Received

↓

OCR

↓

AI Extraction

↓

Business Validation

↓

Human Review

↓

Approved Dataset

↓

Browser Automation

↓

Verification

↓

Completed

```

---

# 6. Business Principles

Every invoice represents one purchase transaction.

Invoice information shall never be invented.

OCR output is not the source of truth.

AI output is not the source of truth.

Approved dataset is the only source of truth.

---

# 7. Required Invoice Fields

| Field | Required |
|---------|----------|
| Invoice Number | Yes |
| Invoice Date | Yes |
| Supplier | Yes |
| Purchase Items | Yes |

---

# 8. Validation Rules

## BR-INV-001

Invoice Number is mandatory.

Missing

↓

Validation Failed.

---

## BR-INV-002

Invoice Number must be unique.

Duplicate

↓

Human Review.

---

## BR-INV-003

Invoice Date is mandatory.

Missing

↓

Validation Failed.

---

## BR-INV-004

Supplier is mandatory.

Missing

↓

Validation Failed.

---

## BR-INV-005

Invoice must contain at least one purchase item.

Zero items

↓

Validation Failed.

---

## BR-INV-006

Each purchase item must satisfy BR-002.

---

## BR-INV-007

Invoice cannot be approved while validation errors exist.

---

## BR-INV-008

Human approval overrides AI extraction.

---

## BR-INV-009

Approved invoices become immutable.

---

## BR-INV-010

Browser Automation shall use only approved invoices.

---

# 9. Human Review

Human Review is required when:

- Duplicate Invoice Number
- Unknown Supplier
- Missing Invoice Date
- Missing Purchase Items
- Validation Failure

Operator actions:

- Approve
- Modify
- Reject

---

# 10. Excel Dataset

One approved invoice produces one Excel record.

Purchase items are stored as invoice details.

Excel must preserve:

- Invoice Number
- Invoice Date
- Supplier
- Purchase Items

---

# 11. Browser Automation

Automation shall:

- Open Purchase Invoice screen.
- Select Supplier.
- Enter Invoice Number.
- Enter Invoice Date.
- Add Purchase Items.
- Save Invoice.

Automation shall never:

- modify approved invoice data
- skip required fields
- create business decisions

---

# 12. Error Conditions

| Code | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| INV-001 | Missing Invoice Number |
| INV-002 | Duplicate Invoice Number |
| INV-003 | Missing Invoice Date |
| INV-004 | Missing Supplier |
| INV-005 | Empty Purchase Items |
| INV-006 | Validation Failed |

---

# 13. Success Criteria

Invoice processing is successful when:

- Invoice passes validation.
- Human review completed (if required).
- Excel dataset generated.
- Browser automation completed.
- Verification completed.

Invoice Status

Completed.
