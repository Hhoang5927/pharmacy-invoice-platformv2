# 1. Purpose

This document defines all business rules related to medicine pricing.

Pricing affects inventory valuation, retail selling price, profitability and regulatory compliance.

Incorrect pricing is considered a critical business error.

---

# 2. Scope

Applies to:

- OCR
- AI Extraction
- Human Review
- Excel Dataset
- Browser Automation

---

# 3. Business Objective

The system shall:

- preserve supplier purchase prices
- correctly identify VAT
- calculate cost consistently
- assist operators in determining retail prices
- prevent incorrect selling prices

---

# 4. Price Data Model

Each medicine contains:

Required

- Purchase Price
- VAT

Optional

- Discount
- Retail Price
- Notes

---

# 5. Business Principles

Purchase Price is extracted from the supplier invoice.

The system shall never invent Purchase Price.

Retail Price may be suggested.

Final Retail Price is approved by the operator.

---

# 6. Validation Rules

## BR-PRI-001

Purchase Price is mandatory.

Missing

↓

Validation Failed.

---

## BR-PRI-002

Purchase Price must be numeric.

Examples

125000

PASS

125.000

PASS (normalized)

ABC

FAIL

---

## BR-PRI-003

Purchase Price must be greater than zero.

Zero

↓

FAIL

Negative

↓

FAIL

---

## BR-PRI-004

VAT is mandatory.

Allowed values:

- 0%
- 5%
- 8%
- 10%

Other values require Human Review.

---

## BR-PRI-005

Discount is optional.

If available

↓

Store exactly.

---

## BR-PRI-006

Retail Price is optional during extraction.

It may be calculated later.

---

## BR-PRI-007

Suggested Retail Price

The system may provide a suggested retail price.

The suggestion is advisory only.

It shall never overwrite operator decisions.

---

## BR-PRI-008

Long Chau Reference

Retail price may be compared with Long Chau.

The comparison is for reference only.

The operator decides the final selling price.

---

## BR-PRI-009

Large price deviation

If

Suggested Price

differs significantly from

Reference Price

↓

Human Review.

---

## BR-PRI-010

Approved Retail Price becomes immutable.

Automation shall use only approved values.

---

# 7. Human Review

Required when:

- Purchase Price missing
- Invalid VAT
- OCR uncertainty
- AI uncertainty
- Large price deviation

Operator may:

- Accept extracted price
- Modify price
- Confirm VAT
- Approve final retail price

---

# 8. Excel Dataset

Approved dataset shall contain:

- Purchase Price
- VAT
- Discount
- Retail Price

All values shall be exported exactly as approved.

---

# 9. Browser Automation

Automation shall:

- Enter Purchase Price
- Enter VAT
- Enter Discount (if available)
- Enter Retail Price (if approved)

Automation shall never:

- calculate prices
- modify prices
- override approved values

---

# 10. Error Conditions

| Code | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| PRI-001 | Missing Purchase Price |
| PRI-002 | Invalid Purchase Price |
| PRI-003 | Invalid VAT |
| PRI-004 | Large Price Difference |
| PRI-005 | Price Validation Failed |

---

# 11. Success Criteria

Price processing is successful when:

- Purchase Price validated
- VAT validated
- Human Review completed (if required)
- Approved dataset generated
- Browser automation completed
