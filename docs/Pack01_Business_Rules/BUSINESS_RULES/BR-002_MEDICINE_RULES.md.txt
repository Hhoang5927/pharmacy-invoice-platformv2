# 1. Purpose

This document defines every business rule related to medicine processing.

Medicine is the core business entity of the Pharmacy Invoice Automation System.

Incorrect medicine information can produce incorrect inventory, incorrect purchasing records and incorrect regulatory data.

These rules ensure every medicine is processed consistently.

---

# 2. Scope

Applies to:

- OCR
- AI Extraction
- Validation
- Human Review
- Excel Dataset
- Browser Automation

---

# 3. Business Objective

The system shall:

- identify medicines correctly
- preserve medicine information
- prevent incorrect medicines
- prevent duplicated medicines
- prevent invalid quantities
- prevent invalid prices

---

# 4. Medicine Data Model

Every medicine contains:

Required

- Medicine Name
- Unit
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- VAT

Optional

- Batch Number
- Expiry Date
- Manufacturer

---

# 5. Medicine Lifecycle

```
OCR

↓

AI Extraction

↓

Validation

↓

Human Review

↓

Approved

↓

Excel Dataset

↓

Browser Automation

↓

Completed
```

---

# 6. Business Principles

Medicine data shall never be invented.

Medicine data shall never be guessed.

Unknown medicines require human review.

Approved medicine data becomes immutable.

Automation uses only approved medicine data.

---

|---------|----------|
