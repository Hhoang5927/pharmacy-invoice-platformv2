# Vision Statement

The Pharmacy Invoice Automation System aims to become a reliable internal platform that automates the processing of pharmaceutical purchase invoices while maintaining a high level of accuracy, transparency and auditability.

The system is designed to reduce repetitive manual work, assist human operators, and support large-scale invoice processing without sacrificing data quality.

Automation should assist humans—not replace business judgment.

---

# Why This Project Exists

Processing supplier invoices manually is one of the most repetitive and time-consuming tasks in pharmacy operations.

A single accounting period may contain:

- Hundreds of purchase invoices
- Thousands of medicine items
- Multiple suppliers
- Different invoice layouts
- Repeated manual verification

Manual processing introduces unnecessary operational cost and increases the probability of human error.

The purpose of this project is to remove repetitive work while preserving human control over business-critical decisions.

---

# Business Vision

The system should allow a pharmacy to process supplier invoices significantly faster than manual methods while maintaining confidence in the generated data.

Instead of typing invoice information manually, users should focus on reviewing exceptions and making business decisions.

Human expertise should be reserved for situations where automation cannot confidently determine the correct result.

---

# Mission

Our mission is:

> Transform invoice processing from manual data entry into an intelligent, review-driven workflow that is accurate, traceable and maintainable.

---

# Guiding Principles

The following principles guide every design decision.

## Accuracy Before Speed

Incorrect data is more expensive than slow processing.

Automation must never prioritize speed over correctness.

---

## Human in Control

The system assists operators.

The system does not replace business decisions.

Whenever confidence is insufficient, human review is required.

---

## Transparency

Every transformation performed by the system should be explainable.

Users should understand where extracted values originated.

---

## Traceability

Every invoice must preserve a complete processing history.

The history should include:

- Original invoice
- OCR result
- AI extraction
- Validation results
- Human modifications
- Automation execution
- Final status

---

## Reliability

The system should continue operating safely even when failures occur.

Unexpected errors should never silently corrupt business data.

---

## Extensibility

The project should support future technologies without requiring large architectural changes.

Examples include:

- Replacing the OCR engine
- Replacing the AI model
- Supporting new pharmacy systems
- Supporting additional suppliers

# Product Philosophy

This project is not an OCR product.

This project is not an AI chatbot.

This project is not a browser automation tool.

Instead, it is an integrated business automation platform that combines several technologies into a single business workflow.

Technology exists to support the workflow—not define it.

---

# Core Design Objectives

The project should be:

Reliable

↓

Predictable

↓

Maintainable

↓

Auditable

↓

Extensible

↓

Efficient

Performance is important, but never at the expense of business correctness.

---

# Business Constraints

The system operates under several important constraints.

## Human Review

Business approval remains mandatory.

Automation should never bypass required human validation.

---

## Invoice Integrity

Original invoices are legal documents.

They must remain unchanged.

---

## Business Data Integrity

Extracted values must accurately represent the invoice.

The system must never invent missing information.

---

## External Dependencies

The system depends on external services including:

- OCR providers
- AI providers
- Pharmacy management website

These dependencies may fail and must be handled gracefully.

---

# Product Scope

The first production version focuses on:

- Supplier invoice processing
- OCR
- AI extraction
- Validation
- Human review
- Excel generation
- Browser automation
- Reporting

Other business functions are intentionally excluded.

---

# Non-Goals

The system is NOT intended to:

- Replace accounting software
- Manage inventory
- Calculate taxes independently
- Predict purchasing decisions
- Perform financial analysis
- Replace pharmacists
- Replace accountants

# Vision for AI

Artificial Intelligence is used to assist structured data extraction.

AI should never become the source of business truth.

Business truth is determined by:

Invoice

↓

Business Rules

↓

Human Validation

AI contributes information but does not determine correctness.

---

# Vision for Automation

Automation exists to execute repetitive actions consistently.

Automation should never hide failures.

Failures should be visible, understandable and recoverable.

---

# Definition of Success

The project will be considered successful when a user can:

- Process hundreds of supplier invoices efficiently.
- Review only exceptions instead of every field.
- Trust the automation results.
- Resume interrupted processing.
- Audit every important action.
- Maintain the system for many years.

---

# Vision Statement Summary

This project exists to transform pharmacy invoice processing into a reliable, intelligent and maintainable workflow that combines automation with human oversight.

The goal is not full autonomy.

The goal is trustworthy automation.
