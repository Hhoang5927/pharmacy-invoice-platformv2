# Purpose

This document defines the mandatory rules that govern the development of the Pharmacy Invoice Automation System.

These rules apply to:

- Human developers
- AI coding assistants
- Future maintainers
- Code reviewers

No implementation may violate these rules unless explicitly approved by the Project Owner.

---

# Rule Hierarchy

The project follows the following priority order.

```
PROJECT_RULES

↓

BUSINESS_RULES

↓

SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE

↓

TECHNICAL_SPECIFICATIONS

↓

IMPLEMENTATION

↓

UNIT TESTS
```

Implementation must always follow higher-level documents.

---

# Core Principles

## PR-001

Business requirements always take precedence over implementation convenience.

---

## PR-002

Correctness is always more important than speed.

Never sacrifice data integrity to gain performance.

---

## PR-003

The system must never fabricate business data.

If information cannot be extracted confidently, it must be flagged for review.

Never guess.

---

## PR-004

Every automated action must be reproducible.

Given the same inputs, the system should produce the same outputs whenever possible.

---

## PR-005

Every important decision must be traceable.

The system must always allow investigation of:

- OCR output
- AI extraction
- Validation
- Human modifications
- Automation
- Final result

---

## PR-010

Documentation is the Single Source of Truth.

---

## PR-011

Code must not redefine business logic.

## PR-012

## PR-013

## PR-014

# Business Rules

## PR-020

Business Rules may only be changed with Project Owner approval.

---

## PR-021

Workflow changes require documentation updates.

---

## PR-022

Business terminology must remain consistent.

Example:

Correct

Purchase Invoice

Incorrect

Invoice

Receipt

Purchase Document

---

## PR-023

Business identifiers must never be renamed without approval.

---

# Architecture Rules

## PR-030

The project follows Clean Architecture.

---

## PR-031

Dependencies point inward only.

---

## PR-032

The Domain Layer must remain independent.

It must not depend on:

- UI
- Database
- Network
- Framework
- AI SDK
- OCR SDK
- Playwright

---

## PR-033

Infrastructure implements Domain interfaces.

Never the opposite.

---

## PR-034

No business logic inside Infrastructure.

---

## PR-040

AI assists business processing.

AI does not define business rules.

---

## PR-041

AI output must always be validated.

---

## PR-042

Low-confidence extraction requires review.

---

## PR-043

AI prompts must be version controlled.

---

## PR-044

Changing prompts requires testing.

# OCR Rules

## PR-050

OCR output is considered raw data.

Never trust OCR without validation.

---

## PR-051

Image preprocessing must never alter business meaning.

---

## PR-052

OCR confidence must be preserved.

---

# Automation Rules

## PR-060

Automation must never continue silently after critical failures.

---

## PR-061

Every automation step must be verifiable.

---

## PR-062

Retries must be controlled.

Infinite retry loops are forbidden.

---

## PR-063

Automation must support resume after interruption.

## PR-071

## PR-072

## PR-074
