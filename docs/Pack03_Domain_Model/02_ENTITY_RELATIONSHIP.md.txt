# 1. Purpose

This document defines the relationships between all business entities in the Pharmacy Invoice Automation Platform.

The objective is to establish a clear ownership model and prevent ambiguous data dependencies.

---

# 2. Design Principles

Relationships shall be:

- Explicit
- Unidirectional where possible
- Business-driven
- Consistent
- Easy to validate
- Independent of storage technology

Circular ownership is prohibited.

---

# 4. Invoice Relationships

Invoice owns:

- Supplier
- Purchase Items
- Invoice Status
- Invoice Totals

Relationship Summary

| Related Entity | Cardinality | Ownership |
|----------------|------------|-----------|
| Supplier | 1 | Reference |
| Purchase Item | 1..N | Owns |
| Approved Dataset | 0..1 | Produces |

---

# 5. Supplier Relationships

Supplier may appear in multiple invoices.

Relationship Summary

| Related Entity | Cardinality |
|----------------|------------|
| Invoice | 0..N |

Supplier is referenced only.

Invoice does not own Supplier master data.

---

# 6. Purchase Item Relationships

Purchase Item belongs to exactly one Invoice.

Purchase Item references exactly one Medicine.

Purchase Item contains exactly one Batch.

Relationship Summary

| Related Entity | Cardinality |
|----------------|------------|
| Invoice | 1 |
| Medicine | 1 |
| Batch | 1 |

---

# 7. Medicine Relationships

Medicine may appear in multiple Purchase Items.

Relationship Summary

| Related Entity | Cardinality |
|----------------|------------|
| Purchase Item | 0..N |

Medicine is reference data.

---

# 8. Batch Relationships

Batch belongs to one Purchase Item.

A Batch cannot exist independently within the business domain.

Relationship Summary

| Related Entity | Cardinality |
|----------------|------------|
| Purchase Item | 1 |

---

# 9. Approved Dataset Relationships

Approved Dataset represents the finalized version of one Invoice.

Relationship Summary

| Related Entity | Cardinality |
|----------------|------------|
| Invoice | 1 |

Approved Dataset is immutable.

---

# 10. Relationship Rules

The following rules apply:

Invoice must contain at least one Purchase Item.

Purchase Item must reference one Medicine.

Purchase Item must contain one Batch.

Invoice must reference one Supplier.

Approved Dataset may only exist after Approval.

---

# 11. Ownership Rules

Invoice owns:

- Purchase Items
- Totals
- Status

Purchase Item owns:

- Quantity
- Unit Price
- VAT
- Batch

Supplier owns:

- Supplier Information

Medicine owns:

- Medicine Information

Approved Dataset owns:

- Final Snapshot

---

# 12. Cascade Rules

Deleting an Invoice removes:

- Purchase Items
- Validation Results
- Review Tasks

Supplier shall not be deleted automatically.

Medicine shall not be deleted automatically.

---

# 13. Reference Integrity

All entity references shall be validated.

Invalid references are prohibited.

Examples:

Purchase Item referencing a missing Medicine is invalid.

Invoice referencing a missing Supplier is invalid.

---

# 14. Aggregate Root

Invoice is the Aggregate Root.

External modules shall interact with:

Invoice

not

Purchase Item

or

Batch

directly.

---

# 15. Future Relationships

The model supports future entities:

Warehouse

Purchase Order

Inventory

Goods Receipt

Sales Invoice

without modifying existing ownership rules.

---

# 16. Success Criteria

The relationship model is successful when:

- Ownership is explicit.
- Cardinality is documented.
- References are valid.
- Aggregate boundaries are respected.
- Future expansion is supported.
